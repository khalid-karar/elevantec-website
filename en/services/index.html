Services

From data to decision, securely and Arabic-first.

Your organization has more data than ever, yet reaching a clear, fast decision is still a challenge.

We help government entities, companies and organizations put AI, data and geospatial analytics to work, and build intelligent systems that support decisions and improve performance. Everything runs in secure, sovereign environments aligned with the National Cybersecurity Authority controls (NCA-ECC), the Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL), and the standards of the National Data Management Office (NDMO) and the Digital Government Authority (DGA), with data hosted inside the Kingdom.
